Point of view

What we actually believe about e-commerce design.

After 8+ years designing at marketplace scale — over 1,000 product detail pages and 2,000 ad banners for Flipkart and Amazon — one pattern is painfully clear: most Indian D2C brands don't have a design problem. They have a conversion problem that happens to look like a design problem. The homepage isn't why customers leave. The third checkout step is.

Founders hire agencies to make things prettier. They should be hiring someone who's seen what actually converts — at the scale where Flipkart and Amazon have already A/B-tested most of the answers. Prettier without converting is expensive decoration.

We bring design judgement calibrated by marketplace-scale experience, paired with AI agents that accelerate the research — so founders get the depth of a senior operator at the speed the work actually needs.

Method

The AIPL framework. Applied specifically to Indian e-commerce.

Every engagement runs through the same four-stage lens — Awareness, Interest, Purchase, Loyalty. Senior human judgement leads the thinking. AI agents accelerate the research — scraping category leaders, benchmarking competitor PDPs, surfacing patterns across thousands of data points — so the strategic work lands in days, not weeks.

A — Awareness

Do they even get here?

Meta and Google ad creative, landing page match, mobile load performance, first-impression trust signals. Most brands over-invest in ad spend and under-invest in the seconds after a click.

I — Interest

Do they stay?

Category architecture, PDP clarity, product photography hierarchy, social proof placement, FAQ friction. The Indian shopper hesitates earlier than the US shopper. Design should anticipate it.

P — Purchase

Do they buy?

Cart UX, checkout step architecture, COD vs prepaid nudges, address form friction, payment method coverage. This is where 70% of conversion revenue is won or lost.

L — Loyalty

Do they return?

Post-purchase experience, order tracking UX, returns clarity, email and WhatsApp touchpoints, repeat-purchase surface design. The second purchase costs a fraction of the first — if the design earns it.

AI in the workflow

We use purpose-built AI agents throughout the engagement — for competitor PDP scraping, pattern analysis across thousands of reviews, category benchmarking, and first-draft research synthesis. The agents accelerate the unglamorous work. The judgement, the recommendations, and the conversations stay human.

It is the difference between four weeks of manual research and four days of augmented research — with the same senior person doing the actual thinking.
